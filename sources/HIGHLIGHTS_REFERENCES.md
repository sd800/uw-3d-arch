# Architectural highlights exterior review

Reviewed 16 October 2026. This revision reviews all **28** tour highlights. Alder Hall is second and keeps its dedicated model. Fifteen other highlights and the separately mapped Henry Art Gallery addition (AVA) are rebuilt where the previous model relied on a shared single-volume facade. The other 13 highlights retain their existing custom geometry after review.

This is a public-source exterior study. Official UW GIS remains the main footprint reference. Secondary massing, window dimensions, floor datums, facade panels, roof profiles and exterior furnishing dimensions are interpretations of photographs and aerials, not surveyed as-built data. Original photography and satellite imagery remain temporary references and are not redistributed.

## Geometry decisions

- Roof skins have positive depth and their own elevations. Pitched roofs are split at the ridge before triangulation; curved monitors use transverse bands so large triangles cannot form false diagonal folds. Glazing, frames, timber edges, solar fins and metal joints remain attached to a wall, slab or roof. Setback terraces also set back their facade members; no full-height loose poles or random rooftop boxes are added.
- The lower Conibear boat-bay datum uses a sample at the mapped lake-facing building edge in the same USGS terrain snapshot. The former central sample described the higher land-entry side and lifted the boat bays too far above the bank. Heights remain approximate; this is not an elevation survey.
- Henry’s original HAG and newer AVA wing retain separate layers. Landmark exploration frames both visible wings together without altering their independent selection/visibility controls.
- Official main footprints, original layer codes, default English, user language switching and current navigation behavior remain in place. Existing detailed housing and Alder geometry are retained.
- `output/highlight_refinement.json` records the 16 rebuilt facility assemblies. `sources/highlights_review.json` records all 28 tour decisions. Targeted export probes test the recessed Kane portico, the open IEB arrival, grounded law-library skylights, roof bounds, unchanged Alder geometry and the second-place tour order and the arena’s continuous roof ridge.
