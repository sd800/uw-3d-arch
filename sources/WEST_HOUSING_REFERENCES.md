# West Campus housing exterior review

Reviewed 15 October 2026. Alder Hall and its dedicated courtyard/street-front builders are excluded from this revision. The 21 affected facilities retain their UW GIS outer envelopes and existing building/layer codes. They are exterior study models. Heights, floor datums, window dimensions, upper setbacks and landscape details are approximate photo/aerial interpretations, not surveyed construction geometry.

## Geometry and ground treatment

- Roof surfaces have their own elevations, with continuous attached parapets and no automatically scattered equipment boxes. Window surrounds and colored infills stay within their own storey; no detached fins or full-building decorative poles are introduced.
- Entry canopies have wall contact and ground-fitted supports. Threshold solids interpolate to nearby pavement; the existing below-grade support geometry remains in place.
- Added paving is restricted to short building approaches and identifiable courts. Mapped roads and walks, the established Elm planting and Alder's precinct remain intact. Planter/bench positions are illustrative and avoid mapped circulation.
- A north-up Esri World Imagery export was reviewed for the west housing district. Its URL and retrieval digest, along with primary page digests, are in `west_housing_review.json`. Image acquisition date is unknown. Photographs and aerial imagery are reference-only and are not included in the published website or delivery package.
- `output/west_housing_refinement.json` records each affected facility, its volume partitions, facade bay count, courts/decks and supported entries.
