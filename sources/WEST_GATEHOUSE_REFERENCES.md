# West Gatehouse review — 2026-10-16

- UW building inventory: facility GH5 / 3928, 3990 West Stevens Way NE; official GIS footprint retained in its original location. The source and OSM both identify it as Gatehouse 5.
- UW Transportation Services exterior photograph: https://transportation.uw.edu/files/media/west-gatehouse-525x384.jpg . Reviewed directly. Dark glazed booth, low shallow gable, purple lower panels and a low brick planter on the entry island.
- Official directions: https://transportation.uw.edu/getting-here/drive . The West Gatehouse is at NE 40th Street / 15th Avenue NE.
- OSM source: https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/361455664 . Separately mapped one-way approaches are ways 361455661 and 361455663; the eastern joining curve is way 459082636.

The former generic one-storey fallback produced a 4.55 m opaque block, while two default 9 m road buffers erased its traffic island. This revision uses the space between the mapped carriageways, with distinct 3.6 m and 6.2 m widths, and rebuilds the booth with an approximate 3.08 m roof peak. Exterior members, exact vertical dimensions, lane widths and planter subdivisions are photo-informed approximations. No operating/open status is asserted; reference photo pixels are not redistributed. The original GIS and OSM snapshots are unchanged.
