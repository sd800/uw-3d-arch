## Alder Hall

- UW HFS exterior and courtyard photos: https://hfs.uw.edu/live/undergraduates/undergraduate-communities/alder-hall/ ; https://hfs.uw.edu/media/0exdnhvw/alder-district-courtyard.jpg ; https://hfs.uw.edu/media/xu0ntwuw/alder-hall.jpg . Reviewed 2026-10-14.
- Mahlum project description confirms 6–7-storey mixed-use residence forms and daylit light courts: https://www.mahlum.com/projects/west-campus-student-housing-phase-i/ . UW PCAD identifies Alder as six storeys and a central light court: https://pcad.lib.washington.edu/building/17794/ .
- Outer envelope: UW GIS. Missing stepped inner court: https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/6447992 , retained in sources/osm_features.geojson. The approximately 618 m² mapped void is removed from all main building/roof masses.
- Ground-level passage, facade bay sizes, heights, canopy supports, courtyard paving/planting and small furniture positions are photo-informed approximations. Original HFS full-floor PDF URL returned 404; it was not used as measured plan evidence.

The immediate Alder precinct retains UW tree/bench positions, official walk routes and OSM street alignments. Narrow entrance aprons meet the flattened hall grade and sampled street terrain continuously. Sidewalk joints are clipped to mapped paving; curbs stop at mapped pedestrian approaches, and tree pits are added only around published trees within paved sidewalks. NE Campus Parkway, Brooklyn Avenue NE, University Way NE and NE 40th Street are the modeled frontage streets. Existing benches in this precinct receive grounded supports and back slats. Widths and module sizes remain illustrative.
