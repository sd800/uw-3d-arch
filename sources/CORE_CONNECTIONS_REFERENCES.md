# Central Plaza approaches and UW Station

Reviewed 16 October 2026.

- [UW campus highlights guide](https://www.washington.edu/visit/campus-highlights-guide/) and its Red Square exterior photograph establish the red brick plaza, stone library entrance landing and surrounding pedestrian spaces. The saved OSM paths retain the actual approach alignments; no second offset route network is overlaid.
- [Suzzallo access guide](https://www.washington.edu/admin/ada/suzzall.htm) identifies the main west entrance from Red Square. [OSM way 1106705315](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1106705315) maps the principal flight with 14 steps and a 10 m width. The centreline locates the approach and its 14-step count is retained. The route width is not treated as the full architectural stair width. The [GLY full west elevation and entrance detail](https://www.gly.com/uw-seismic-improvements), together with the [UW Libraries tour](https://uw.pressbooks.pub/uwlibrariestour/chapter/suzzallo-library-4/), control the broad short central flight, continuous raised west terrace, brick/stone paving, open balustrades, three recessed portals, thirteen upper window bays and side ramp. Stair widths (21.6 m at top, 24.4 m at bottom), a 4.5 m tread run, the 0.16 m illustrative rise and secondary members are photo-interpreted approximations. Explore faces this west front from the west with a slight southern offset. The lower forecourt uses flush brick courses, with closed flared stone stair cheeks. Lower apron vertices take precedence over the adjacent raised terrace during terrain interpolation, preventing false paving wedges. The previous whole-library centroid grade was below the plaza despite the mapped upward entry; the west entry now establishes the simplified library grade. This is a local display correction to the coarse DEM, not a survey height.
- The surrounding saved OSM pedestrian routes and stair ways define the approaches to Kane, Odegaard, Meany, Gerberding and the Quad. Exterior entrance shelves blend back into sampled terrain. Tagged counts are retained; untagged counts are recorded as estimates. Finer local terrain patches resolve the short grade transitions without changing the source DEM.
- [Meany Center exterior photograph](https://meanycenter.org/visit/visitor-information) shows opaque brick performance enclosures and gray sloping roof sections. [UW Magazine](https://magazine.washington.edu/theater-landscape-to-be-dramatically-different/) documents metal replacing the sloping roof sections. Meany receives moderate volume/roof/lobby optimization. Kane, Odegaard, Gerberding and the adjacent Quad halls retain their existing individual architecture assemblies.
- [LMN Architects: University of Washington Station](https://lmnarchitects.com/project/university-of-washington-station-sound-transit) documents the two-level glass/steel entrance, thin rectangular upper roof, lower canopy projections and connected bridge. The mapped outer envelope contains multiple floor levels; it must not be extruded wholesale as one upper roof. The intermediate circulation opening, simplified stair/escalator envelopes, open portals and exterior stair landing are corrected. Underground railway construction is not modeled. Old tree points conflicting with the station bridge/stair walking corridor are omitted from this surface display. The adjacent plaza is locally graded so the coarse DEM cannot fill the doors.

Reference photography is used for interpretation only; image pixels are not redistributed. Mapped footprints remain unchanged. Secondary dimensions, roof partitions, terrain transitions and members are independent exterior approximations.

## Entrance follow-up — 16 October 2026

The GLY entrance detail photograph shows paired inner lancets inside each of the three deep pointed portals, stone tracery and a figure supported by the central trumeau. These now have separate inset door frames, restrained handles, inner arches, round tracery and small supported figure/canopy silhouettes. The figures are schematic exterior masses, not copies of the carved artworks.

The entrance domain is removed before general terrain triangulation and given a lower closed ground base; the level terrace and apron are separate brick assemblies. This prevents interpolated ground facets crossing the stair risers and appearing as white triangles beneath the terrace balustrade. Ground is included in the exported apron ray checks. Explore uses direction [-1, -0.015, 0.70] (east/north/up axes), matching the supplied almost-due-west front view with Red Square foreground. Secondary dimensions remain photo-interpreted approximations.
